What is AI JailBreak?

AI JailBreak is a common term for prompts that try to change an AI model’s usual behavior, role or response style. At JailBrok, it describes a collection of prompts organized into folders with their own instructions.

Prompts, context and roles

A regular prompt describes a task, such as preparing an outline or explaining a piece of code. An AI jailbreak prompt may also specify a role, conversation format or additional context. Results depend on the model, its version and the service’s settings. A prompt does not change the model’s software or weights.

02

How JailBrok is organized

Select an AI icon on the homepage. With an active subscription, you can open its folders to find material names, instructions, prompt text and a TXT file when provided. The free trial prompt lets you explore how materials are delivered before purchasing a subscription.

03

Prompts for different AI models

The library has sections for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Antigravity, Grok, DeepSeek, Kimi, Qwen, GLM, Perplexity and Cursor. Folders and instructions may be updated. A prompt for one model may behave differently in another, so check the material’s name and instructions first.

04

What to keep in mind

A prompt cannot guarantee the removal of every restriction or make AI answers error-free. Model updates can change results. Do not put passwords, payment details or other people’s personal information into chats. Use materials in accordance with the law and the rules of the AI service you choose.
