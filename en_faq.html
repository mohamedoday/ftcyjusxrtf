Questions about AI JailBreak and JailBrok

Here are answers about the website, accounts and access to prompts. For help with a particular folder, start with its instructions or contact support.

01

Is there a free AI JailBreak prompt?

Yes. The homepage has a free trial prompt button. Sign-in is required to receive it, and the ready result can be opened again.

02

Does my bot subscription work on the website?

Yes, when you sign in with the same Telegram account linked to the subscription. Signing into another account will not identify that subscription.

03

How do I sign up with email?

Enter your registration details and the six-digit email code. The account is created only after confirmation. If the message is missing, check Spam and the resend option on the screen.

04

Can I use it on mobile and desktop?

Yes. The website adapts to mobile screens. Language, color and motion preferences sync within one account; desktop and mobile can keep separate Telegram sessions.

05

Does this include a ChatGPT or Claude subscription?

No. JailBrok provides its own library of prompts and instructions. Accounts and plans for your chosen AI service are managed separately with that provider.

06

Will every AI give the same result?

No. Results depend on the model, version, context and service updates. Use the instructions for your chosen folder and verify AI responses.

07

Where can I check prices and payment methods?

Select the subscription banner and open the payment options. The current amount, currency and methods appear before an invoice is created. Payment status is checked with the Telegram bot.

08

How do I contact support?

Use Tech support on the homepage or the link at the bottom of this page. Include your account ID and describe the problem. Never share your password or one-time codes.
