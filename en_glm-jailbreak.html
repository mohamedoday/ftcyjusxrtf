Planning and step-by-step requirements

For GLM, separate the goal, inputs, constraints and output format. If a task has several parts, list the expected result for each one. Supply facts that must remain unchanged in business writing, and ask specific questions when requesting an analysis.

02

How to evaluate the result

Check completeness against your requirements list. Attractive formatting does not mean every part of the task was completed. Choose a file matching the GLM version described in its instructions. Change one requirement at a time when refining the request.

03

Where to find a file and instructions

Open JailBrok, choose GLM and read the material's description. Subscription files require sign-in and access verification.
