How to use JailBrok

This guide takes you from your first sign-in to opening a prompt. JailBrok provides prompts and instructions; you continue using your chosen AI in that AI service’s own chat.

On this page

01

1. Sign in to your account

Open My account and choose Telegram or email registration. Email registration requires the six-digit code sent to your inbox. For Telegram, start on the website and confirm in the bot. Open the confirmation link in the same browser where you started; if Telegram opens another browser, copy the link into the original one.

02

2. Choose a trial prompt or subscription material

The free trial prompt button is in the center of the homepage. For subscription materials, select your AI icon and choose a folder. If the icons show padlocks, check your sign-in and subscription status in My account.

03

3. Read the instructions and open your AI chat

Read all the instructions first. Then copy the prompt or download the TXT file when available, and follow the steps in your chosen AI service. Once the chat is ready, describe your task, desired format and context. Check important facts in the response.

04

Your subscription on the website and Telegram

Your Telegram bot subscription is recognized when you sign in with the same Telegram account. The website offers SBP, X-Rocket and cryptocurrency; available methods and the amount are shown before payment. The server verifies subscription status, and My account shows the time remaining. A JailBrok subscription does not include the AI providers’ own paid plans.

05

If a material does not open

Check that you are signed into the right account and that your subscription is active, then reload the page. On another device, sign into the same account: desktop and mobile sessions can work independently. If the issue continues, contact support with your account ID, AI provider and folder name. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes or payment credentials.
