Long documents and source comparison

For Kimi, define which materials should be compared and what conclusion you need. With multiple documents, ask the same questions of each source. Separate points of agreement, disagreements and statements that are not supported by the material you supplied.

02

How to evaluate the result

Confirm that the relevant document sections are available in your chosen interface. Summaries may omit exceptions, so compare important conclusions with the originals. Evaluate alternative prompts using the same material and a consistent set of quality criteria.

Where to find a file and instructions
