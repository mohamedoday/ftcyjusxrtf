Context and structured answers

For a Gemini task, list the available inputs and explain the result you need. If your interface accepts images or other attachments, identify which details matter. When comparing alternatives, use consistent criteria and ask the model to distinguish observations from assumptions.

02

How to evaluate the result

Match the prompt to the model name and the usage method in its instructions. The same request can behave differently across interfaces. Check that relevant attachments were considered, the requested format was followed and assumptions were not presented as verified facts.
