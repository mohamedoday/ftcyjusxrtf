Ideas, style and checking claims

For Grok, describe the style you need: business writing, a short explanation, a script or several alternative ideas. Identify the audience and tone constraints. For factual or current-event tasks, distinguish checkable information from opinions and creative assumptions.

02

How to evaluate the result

Compare outputs by how well they solve your task, not just by how bold they sound. Verify dates, links and quotations separately. If an answer is too generic, provide an example and clear quality criteria. Select a library file that matches your Grok model version.

03

Where to find a file and instructions

Open JailBrok, choose Grok and read the material's description. Subscription files require sign-in and access verification.
